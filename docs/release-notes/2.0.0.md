SRT AI Translator 2.0 is a complete rewrite: a much smaller download, three ways to translate (cloud AI, AI on your own PC, or fully offline) and cleaner subtitles.

![SRT AI Translator 2.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MaloneFreak/SRTAITranslate/master/docs/screenshot.png)

## Download

Download **`SRTAI_Translator.exe`** below and run it. No installation, no Python, no Hugging Face token.

> Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the file against `SRTAI_Translator.exe.sha256`.

## Highlights

**50 MB instead of 2.5 GB.** Version 1.4 came as two split archives of about 1.2 GB each (with PyTorch inside) and then downloaded a 17 GB model. 2.0 is a single `.exe`, and models are downloaded only when you choose them, with progress and resume.

**Choose your engine**
- **Cloud AI:** Google Gemini (free tier), OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, DeepL, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or any OpenAI-compatible server.
- **Local AI:** Ollama (the app downloads the models for you, including Google's TranslateGemma) and LM Studio.
- **Offline:** NLLB-200 600M, 1.3B or 3.3B (0.6, 1.4 or 3.4 GB), on the CPU or an NVIDIA GPU. The 3.3B is the model used by 1.4, now about 5x smaller and faster.

**Better subtitles**
- AI engines see the surrounding dialogue, and every line is checked; missing lines are retried.
- Sentences split across two subtitles are translated as one sentence and split back (offline and DeepL).
- Keeps italics, `{\an8}` positions, dialogue dashes and music notes, and re-balances line breaks (42 characters per line).
- Detects the source language and the file encoding automatically.
- Glossary, notes about the video, formality, bilingual subtitles and removal of hearing-impaired notes.

**Easier to use**
- New interface with dark and light themes, in English and Portuguese (Brazil).
- Drag and drop many files or whole folders, with a live preview of each translated line.
- Saves `Movie.pt-BR.srt` next to the original, so video players load it automatically.
- Translation memory: an interrupted job continues where it stopped, and repeated lines are not paid twice.
- API keys are stored in the Windows Credential Manager.
- Command line for batch jobs (see the README).

## Getting started

1. Add your `.srt` files.
2. Pick an engine. The quickest start is **Google Gemini** with a free key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey). For no internet at all, choose **Offline**: the model downloads the first time.
3. Pick the target language and click **Translate**.

## Upgrading from 1.x

- 2.0 does not use the old files. You can delete `SRTAI_Translator.zip` / `.z01` and the 1.x model cache (usually `C:\Users\<you>\.cache\huggingface`) to free about 20 GB.
- Translated files are now saved next to the original as `name.<language>.srt`, instead of `Downloads\translated_name.srt`.
- Languages are chosen from a list: no more codes like `por_Latn`.

## Good to know

- The NLLB offline models are licensed CC-BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial use).
- With cloud engines, the subtitle text is sent to the provider you choose. Local and offline engines keep everything on your PC.
